Are roll bar grab handles safe on all Jeep Wranglers?

Not all styles. JL/JLU (2018+) and Gladiator (2020+) roll bars contain SRS airbags. You must use bolt-on handles on these vehicles. JK, TJ, and YJ Wranglers do not have roll bar airbags and can use any style.

What is the best material for roll bar handles?

550 paracord (Type III military paracord) is the gold standard — UV-resistant, rot-resistant, rated to 550 lbs. Aluminum billet handles are a good alternative for a more tactical look. Avoid cheap webbing or rubber handles.
